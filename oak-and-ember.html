Our Story

Cooked the slow way, on purpose.

Oak & Ember began with a simple idea: the best American food is made with patience. Our open hearth burns local oak all day, and everything that leaves our kitchen has touched flame, from the bread to the bourbon-glazed carrots.

We work directly with farmers, ranchers, and fishermen within a few hours of our door, so the menu changes with the seasons and the flavors stay honest. Pull up a chair, stay a while, and let us take care of the rest.
